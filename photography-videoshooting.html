Behind The Scenes

From The First Shot
To The Final Frame.

Every outstanding visual begins with a process. We plan the story, prepare the scene, capture the details and refine every frame.

STEP 01

01. Plan & Prepare

We discuss your goals, develop a creative direction, create shot lists, scout locations and plan lighting, equipment and production schedules.

STEP 02

02. Shoot & Direct

We capture photographs and video using considered compositions, camera movement, lighting setups, multiple angles and professional direction.

STEP 03

03. Edit & Deliver

We select the strongest takes, refine the pacing, grade the colours, mix the audio, add motion graphics and export content for your platforms.
